NATIONAL GREEN MANUFACTURING & TAX INCENTIVE ACT 2025
Document Ref: NGMTIA-2025-SEC12B | Jurisdiction: Federal / Ministry of Finance
Clause 12b Capital Allowances & Production Subsidies
Industrial production facilities achieving an Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) of 80.0% or greater and
maintaining carbon intensity per product unit below 0.450 kg CO2/unit are eligible for candidate review
under green manufacturing tax incentive categories. Unverified proxy data requires external tax professional review.
